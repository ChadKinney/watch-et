How to Clean Your Watch Without Scratching It

October 20, 2026

Sweat, dust, sunscreen, and salt water work their way into every crevice of a watch. Left alone, that grime dulls the finish and wears on seals and straps. A simple cleaning routine takes a few minutes and keeps your watch looking new for years. Here is the right way to do it.

Start with clean water

Rinse the watch under lukewarm tap water to wash off loose grit. Grit is the enemy here. Wiping a dry, dusty watch drags tiny particles across the finish like sandpaper. Get it wet first, every single time, before anything touches the case.

Use a soft cloth, nothing rough

A microfiber cloth and a drop of mild soap handle almost everything. Work around the case, the lugs, and the case back where sweat collects. For tight spots, a soft toothbrush lifts grime from around the crown and between bracelet links. Gentle pressure is plenty. If you are scrubbing hard, you are doing it wrong.

Know your strap

Metal bracelets take soap and water without complaint. Rubber and nylon straps do too. Leather is the exception. Soak leather and it stains, stiffens, and wears out fast. Clean a leather strap with a barely damp cloth and let it air dry on its own, never with heat.

Dry it fully

Water hiding under the lugs and around the case back causes trouble over time. Pat the watch dry with a clean towel, then let it air dry the rest of the way before you put it away. Do not use a hair dryer. Heat and watches are a bad mix, and a blast of hot air can push moisture deeper into places you cannot reach.

What to avoid

Skip bleach, harsh solvents, and abrasive pads. They strip finishes and eat through coatings faster than you would believe. Skip the ultrasonic cleaner for any watch you care about unless you know exactly what you are doing. Plain soap and water beats aggressive chemistry every time.

The best cleaning routine is the one you rarely need. A WATCH-ET sleeve keeps sweat, dust, and grime off your watch in the first place, so the quick scrub stays a sometime thing instead of a weekly chore. Less cleaning, fewer scratches, more time wearing the watch.
