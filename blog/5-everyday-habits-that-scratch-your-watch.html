5 Everyday Habits That Scratch Your Watch

October 6, 2026

Most watch damage does not come from one big accident. It comes from small daily hits that add up until the case looks tired and the crystal collects marks. Here are the five we see most, and what to do about each one.

1. Typing with the watch grinding the desk

Eight hours a day, the edge of your watch case kisses the desk every time you reach for the mouse. Over months that polishes flat spots into the finish. Fix: slide the watch up your wrist an inch while you work, or rest your wrist on a pad.

2. Door frames, gym racks, and tailgates

One careless swing through a doorway or one bump against a weight rack leaves a mark you will see every day. Fix: lead with your other arm in tight spaces, and take the watch off before lifting.

3. Yard work and tools

Shovels, wrenches, ladders, and lumber are all harder than your watch case. An afternoon of chores can do more damage than a year at the office. Fix: pocket the watch or leave it inside before you start.

4. Tossing it in with keys and coins

The nightstand drawer, the gym bag pocket, the truck cupholder. Anywhere your watch rattles around loose with metal, it loses. Fix: give it its own spot, a pouch, a stand, or even a sock in a pinch.

5. Laying it face down on hard surfaces

Setting your watch face down on a counter or nightstand drags the crystal across grit you cannot see. Fix: lay it on its side or on a soft cloth, every single time.

The common thread: almost all of it happens while you are busy living your life, not while you are thinking about the watch.
