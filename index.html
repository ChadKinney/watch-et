How It Works

On in seconds. Protection all day.

  1. Slide it on. The neoprene sleeve slips over your watch and wrist.
  2. Flip the cover. The padded flap covers the watch face to help guard against scratches and knocks.
  3. Stash your essentials. The side pocket holds cash, a key, or small gear.
  4. Get after it. Work, hike, fish, ride, your watch stays protected.

Most watches are water resistant but not scratch proof. The WATCH-ET™ helps keep yours looking new.

Our Story

Veteran owned. Invented to protect.

The WATCH-ET™ Wristwatch Protector was invented by U.S. veteran Joseph C. Kinney to guard wristwatches and now smartwatches from the knocks of everyday adventure.

WATCH-ET (DBA Adventurers Neoprene Watch Protection) is a U.S. veteran owned company based in Mobile, Alabama. Every sleeve is built around a simple idea: your watch works hard. It deserves protection that works as hard as you do.

  • Neoprene sleeve designed to cushion everyday knocks
  • Cover flap covers the crystal and face
  • Side pocket for cash, keys & small items
  • Hook and loop closure fits a range of wrists
